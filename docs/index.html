Overview

A closer look.

Liquid Glass

Glass that bends the light.

Every bar, button and panel is frosted glass with real edge refraction. The whole window takes a soft tint from your wallpaper.

Pages

The web, framed.

Sites sit in a rounded frame beside a calm sidebar, with pinned tabs above and today's tabs below.

Dark

Beautiful after dark.

Liquid Glass Dark turns everything down without losing the glass. Each Space can have its own look.

Search

Search that keeps up.

Suggestions as you type, from the home screen or a Safari-style top bar. Your favourite engine, one click to switch.

Tab Overview

Every tab, in 3D.

Press Ctrl Shift A and your tabs line up as live pictures in a Cover Flow carousel. Arrow keys to browse, Enter to open.

Settings

Make it yours.

Dozens of themes, fonts, accent colours, window materials and layouts. Change anything, see it instantly.

Welcome

Hello, Gallium.

The first time you open it, drops of liquid metal gather into one, a mirror of your wallpaper, drawn live in 3D.

Best with sound on. Every sound is made live, no audio files.

Set up in seconds.

Three quick choices, each one shown live behind the card.

  • 1
    Your look. Liquid Glass, Liquid Glass Dark, Apple or Apple Dark.
  • 2
    A wallpaper. Pick a photo for your home screen.
  • 3
    Search. Your engine, plus a top bar or an Arc-style sidebar.

Features

All the little things.

Spaces

Separate sets of tabs, each with its own theme. Swipe between them.

Pinned tabs

The sites you use every day, always one click away.

Chrome extensions

Install from the Chrome Web Store, open them from the toolbar.

Downloads

Live progress, cancel any time, open in one click.

Mute tab

Silence any noisy tab right from the sidebar.

Undo

Closed a tab or deleted a Space by mistake? Bring it right back.

DevTools

The full Chromium developer tools, plus find in page.

Light on memory

Tabs you aren't using unload themselves and wake up when you return.

Themes & Mods

Make it yours.

Design a theme in the Theme Studio and watch the whole browser change as you go. Or pick one from the gallery, where every file is checked and virus-scanned.
