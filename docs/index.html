Everything in its place

The parts of Arc you love, rebuilt for Windows with Apple-style polish.

Spaces & pinned tabs

A vertical sidebar with favourites, pinned tabs and Today tabs, grouped into Spaces you switch between with a 3D cube turn.

Liquid Glass

Frosted glass with real edge refraction, plus light, dark and Apple themes. Mica and Acrylic window materials.

Photo wallpapers

Real landscapes and abstracts on the home screen, with the window tinted by a soft gradient taken from the photo.

Safari-style search

Search right from the toolbar or the home screen, with grouped suggestions, history and recently closed tabs.

Tab Overview

See every tab at once in a Cover Flow carousel with live snapshots. Hover a tab for a preview.

Chrome extensions

Install extensions from the Chrome Web Store and open their popups right from the toolbar.

Real Chromium tools

Downloads you can cancel, find in page, DevTools, mute tab, and a custom right-click menu.

Undo everything

Closed a tab or deleted a Space by mistake? One click on Undo brings it right back.

Small and fast

Built with Tauri and Microsoft WebView2. A 17 MB download, with unused tabs unloaded to save memory.

Browse like liquid.

Free for Windows 10 and 11.
