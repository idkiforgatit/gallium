Liquid Glass

Glass that bends the light.

Every bar, button and panel is frosted glass with real edge refraction. The window takes its tint from your wallpaper, in light or dark.

Real refraction

Edges bend what's behind them, like a thick pane of glass. Not just a blur.

Tinted by your photo

The window picks up a soft gradient from your wallpaper, so it always matches.

26 wallpapers

Landscapes and abstracts built in, or use your own picture.

Welcome

Hello, Gallium.

The first time you open it, drops of liquid metal fall together into one, drawn live in 3D, with soft sounds to match.

Set up in seconds.

Three quick choices, and you'll see each one take effect behind the card.

  • 1
    Your look. Liquid Glass, Liquid Glass Dark, Apple or Apple Dark.
  • 2
    A wallpaper. Pick a photo for your home screen.
  • 3
    Search. Your engine, and a Safari-style top bar or an Arc-style sidebar.

Features

Everything in its place.

Every tab, in 3D.

Press Ctrl Shift A to see your tabs as live pictures in a Cover Flow carousel. Browse with the arrow keys and open one with Enter.

Search that keeps up.

Suggestions as you type, from the home screen or the toolbar.

Make it yours.

Dozens of themes, fonts, accent colours and layouts.

Spaces

Separate sets of tabs, each with its own theme. Swipe between them.

Pinned tabs

Keep the sites you use every day one click away.

Chrome extensions

Install from the Chrome Web Store and open them from the toolbar.

Downloads

Live progress, cancel any time, open in one click.

Mute tab

Silence any noisy tab right from the sidebar.

Undo

Closed a tab or deleted a Space by mistake? Bring it right back.

DevTools

The full Chromium developer tools, plus find in page.

Light on memory

Built on Tauri and WebView2. Unused tabs unload themselves.
